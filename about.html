Guiding Directives

The Four Non-Negotiable Tenets

01. Zero-Friction Hand-off

Designers who write production code and engineers who understand typography eliminate hand-off ambiguity permanently.

02. Performance as Aesthetic

A 60fps interaction that loads in 18ms is inherently more beautiful than an over-engineered asset with layout shift.

03. Architectural Longevity

Prioritize zero-build vanilla web fundamentals that remain deployable for decades without fragile dependency churn.

04. Humanistic Affirmation

Software should respect human cognition, preserve mental calm, and provide tactile delight in everyday digital tools.
